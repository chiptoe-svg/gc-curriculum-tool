## Conflicts and uncertainties found while drafting

1. **Alumni base and figures differ between documents.** AGC §2 (stamp 77cb46d1d20a5dd1, 7 Oct): framework shares 64.0/17.1/15.6/3.3 of 642 first jobs = 97.0% of **662**. AGC §3 target classification: n = **661** (19 more in re-review); AGC §7 cites that classification as gc_alumni commit f545652 (6 Oct) while the §2 caption cites commit cb39913. BG §16 and GO (snapshot 2026-10-05): n = **672** first jobs, shares 64.6/16.9/14.7/3.8, and "about 96%" vs AGC's "about 97%" in change-expected categories. Pages use only AGC's figures (661 split; 642 of 662 for the OpenAI join) with the commits the author specified (f545652 for the 661 classification, cb39913 for the data-stamp/framework-join provenance); no 672 or 64.6/16.9/14.7/3.8 figures appear anywhere; each page's alumni note says the figures may be refreshed when the alumni roster matching settles. 2026-10-14 correction: also normalized "one coder"/"single coder" wording across all seven pages to the papers' phrasing ("descriptive until the independent second-coder check" / "has not yet had its independent second-coder check").
2. **Entry-rung gap wording.** AGC §1: "19% relative employment gap" (per the August 2026 Canaries revision the paper cites). BG §16: "about 18 percentage points behind" (the Background's own figure, from an earlier Canaries vintage — see background.html note below). Pages use AGC's "19%" throughout; no "18 percentage points" appears anywhere in the wiki pages.
3. **Judgment framing.** AGC §5 states "Treat judgment as the scarce skill" as a recommendation; BG §16 calls the same idea "a hypothesis to test, not a result" and notes the payroll study's tacit-knowledge result partly cuts the other way. 2026-10-14 correction: pages now state explicitly, wherever this claim is presented, that "the paper proposes treating judgment as the scarce skill. The support is two workplace studies and suggestive, non-causal payroll evidence, not a tested result," and keep the note that BG treats it as a hypothesis (ai-careers-brief, areas-of-focus).
4. **Coverage currency.** AGC §4's table reflects five courses scored in June; EB reports 19 of 59 captured (9 Oct); CC §04 reports 23 snapshots re-scored after the October merges. The pages present the AGC table with its date and say counts await a full rerun.
5. **Graphic designer elasticity** 1.45 (report chart) vs 1.08 (data file): the paper's own flagged discrepancy; pages state both.
6. **Specialty courses.** AGC §4 says 14 specialty courses sit outside the career-target matrix; CC §06 (October) says every program-visible course now counts toward career totals, while matrix rows show only career-path courses. The page says "sat outside ... when the paper was written".
