## 1. Initial synthesis (first research pass — the ADVERSARIAL TARGET)

An initial Opus research pass (reading `CLAUDE.md`, `depth-scale.md`, `background.html` §4/§6/§7/§8/§16 + the literature) produced these claims. The deep dive exists to **test, ground, and challenge** them, not to accept them.

**Landscape.** Polanyi ("we know more than we can tell") — tacit is a *dimension of all knowing*, not a separate filing cabinet. **Collins** splits it into **relational** (contingently un-articulated, codifiable), **somatic** (embodied hand/eye skill), and **collective** (community-embedded, the only "strong"/un-externalizable kind) — and argues *most* apparently-tacit knowledge is contingently tacit and codifiable. The operator's "explicit→tacit" claim is literally **Nonaka & Takeuchi's SECI *Internalization*** (learning by doing); the mechanistically respectable version underneath it is **Anderson's ACT-R proceduralization** (declarative→procedural knowledge compilation → automatic, un-narrated skill), and **Dreyfus & Dreyfus** novice→expert (experts act on intuition they can't fully articulate). The *reverse* conversion (tacit→explicit) is **Externalization** — Schön/Kolb reflection.

**Testable claims (each flagged validates / refines / challenges KUD+):**
1. **Explicit/tacit maps onto KUD — REFINES.** Know ≈ explicit/declarative (clean fit). Do's upper anchors (D4–D5: adapt to novel conditions, perform creatively, guide others) ≈ tacit/proceduralized expertise. Understand is *split* — U1–U3 explicit, U4–U5 shade into expert intuition (tacit). So the explicit→tacit gradient runs **diagonally up the depth scale** (low depth = explicit/rule-bound; high U & D = tacit), not across the K/U/D columns; K stays explicit at all depths.
2. **The KUD+ dissociation cases ARE tacit-knowledge phenomena — VALIDATES (strongest claim).** "D-high/U-low = craft without articulation" = tacit skill that ran Internalization but not Externalization (Polanyi's cyclist; the expert who can't verbalize). "U-high/D-low = theory without craft" = explicit knowledge stuck in ACT-R's declarative stage, not yet proceduralized. The dissociations are "the SECI conversions caught mid-cycle, viewed as deficits."
3. **Experiential learning is the explicit→tacit engine — VALIDATES mechanism, REFINES framing.** SECI-Internalization ≈ proceduralization ≈ Dreyfus progression, all landing on the Do 3→4→5 climb. BUT the operator's claim is *half a loop*: the framework's own §4 engine (depth → productive failure → **structured reflection**) requires the tacit→explicit return trip; failure without consolidation runs Internalization but skips Externalization → produces exactly the D-high/U-low deficit. Act 2 ("integration & mastery," the mid-Junior "aha," GC 4400/4060) is where compilation/internalization completes (the D3→D4 / competent→proficient jump).
4. **Tacit/proceduralized knowledge is more durable — VALIDATES §6 strongly.** Procedural memory is markedly more decay-resistant than declarative; same shape as Bahrick's permastore (already in §6). Implication: durability should decay **K faster than D**, with permastore-like slow tails on reinforced D4–D5 — i.e. **axis is itself a durability signal**, which the current §6 (decay by reinforcement pattern, not axis) doesn't encode.
5. **THE KEY TENSION — does an evidence-from-artifacts tool under-measure tacit expertise? PARTIALLY / honestly yes at one seam.** Rebuttal: the Do axis operationalizes tacit fluency **behaviorally** (performance under novelty, guides others) — the standard expert–novice move (infer tacit competence from performance, never ask for articulation), so the tool does *not* broadly under-measure it. BUT a real, admitted, tacitness-correlated bias remains at exactly one seam: **somatic/craft** competencies assessed through **text-poor** channels (studio critique, observed performance, portfolio) — §7 already confesses this. And **collective/strong** tacit (community judgment) is un-capturable in principle — but the framework already relocates it to foundational dispositions (D-only) + the program-level emergent property, rather than scoring it per course.

**Four proposed doc-implications (to be tested for justification/overreach):**
1. Make the tacit-fluency reading of D4–D5 explicit in §4/§6.
2. Elevate the §7 text-poor-assessment bias to a named "tacit-knowledge measurement limit" + tie the instructor-confirmation mitigation to it; flag craft courses as priority for the non-text probe.
3. Let *axis* inform the durability model (§6/§15 hyp. B): decay K faster than D.
4. Prioritize "performance under novel/varied conditions" evidence (also the hardest for AI to counterfeit — §16).

**Honesty caveats from pass 1:** SECI is a *management heuristic, not validated cognitive science* (Gourlay critique) — lean on ACT-R/Dreyfus for mechanism, cite SECI only as vocabulary. The operator's claim is one-directional (omits the reflection return-trip §4 requires). The Q5 rebuttal rests on an *inference* from behavior (inherits §7's keyword-inference + text-poor biases) — a good answer to the tension, not a dissolution of it.
