## 1. Initial synthesis (first research pass — the ADVERSARIAL TARGET)

An initial Opus research pass (reading `CLAUDE.md`, `depth-scale.md`, `background.html` §4/§6/§7/§8/§16 + the literature) produced these claims. The deep dive exists to **test, ground, and challenge** them, not to accept them.

**Landscape.** Polanyi ("we know more than we can tell") — tacit is a *dimension of all knowing*, not a separate filing cabinet. **Collins** splits it into **relational** (contingently un-articulated, codifiable), **somatic** (embodied hand/eye skill), and **collective** (community-embedded, the only "strong"/un-externalizable kind) — and argues *most* apparently-tacit knowledge is contingently tacit and codifiable. The operator's "explicit→tacit" claim is literally **Nonaka & Takeuchi's SECI *Internalization*** (learning by doing); the mechanistically respectable version underneath it is **Anderson's ACT-R proceduralization** (declarative→procedural knowledge compilation → automatic, un-narrated skill), and **Dreyfus & Dreyfus** novice→expert (experts act on intuition they can't fully articulate). The *reverse* conversion (tacit→explicit) is **Externalization** — Schön/Kolb reflection.

**Testable claims (each flagged validates / refines / challenges KUD+):**
1. **Explicit/tacit maps onto KUD — REFINES.** Know ≈ explicit/declarative (clean fit). Do's upper anchors (D4–D5: adapt to novel conditions, perform creatively, guide others) ≈ tacit/proceduralized expertise. Understand is *split* — U1–U3 explicit, U4–U5 shade into expert intuition (tacit). So the explicit→tacit gradient runs **diagonally up the depth scale** (low depth = explicit/rule-bound; high U & D = tacit), not across the K/U/D columns; K stays explicit at all depths.
2. **The KUD+ dissociation cases ARE tacit-knowledge phenomena — VALIDATES (strongest claim).** "D-high/U-low = craft without articulation" = tacit skill that ran Internalization but not Externalization (Polanyi's cyclist; the expert who can't verbalize). "U-high/D-low = theory without craft" = explicit knowledge stuck in ACT-R's declarative stage, not yet proceduralized. The dissociations are "the SECI conversions caught mid-cycle, viewed as deficits."
3. **Experiential learning is the explicit→tacit engine — VALIDATES mechanism, REFINES framing.** SECI-Internalization ≈ proceduralization ≈ Dreyfus progression, all landing on the Do 3→4→5 climb. BUT the operator's claim is *half a loop*: the framework's own §4 engine (depth → productive failure → **structured reflection**) requires the tacit→explicit return trip; failure without consolidation runs Internalization but skips Externalization → produces exactly the D-high/U-low deficit. Act 2 ("integration & mastery," the mid-Junior "aha," GC 4400/4060) is where compilation/internalization completes (the D3→D4 / competent→proficient jump).
4. **Tacit/proceduralized knowledge is more durable — VALIDATES §6 strongly.** Procedural memory is markedly more decay-resistant than declarative; same shape as Bahrick's permastore (already in §6). Implication: durability should decay **K faster than D**, with permastore-like slow tails on reinforced D4–D5 — i.e. **axis is itself a durability signal**, which the current §6 (decay by reinforcement pattern, not axis) doesn't encode.
5. **THE KEY TENSION — does an evidence-from-artifacts tool under-measure tacit expertise? PARTIALLY / honestly yes at one seam.** Rebuttal: the Do axis operationalizes tacit fluency **behaviorally** (performance under novelty, guides others) — the standard expert–novice move (infer tacit competence from performance, never ask for articulation), so the tool does *not* broadly under-measure it. BUT a real, admitted, tacitness-correlated bias remains at exactly one seam: **somatic/craft** competencies assessed through **text-poor** channels (studio critique, observed performance, portfolio) — §7 already confesses this. And **collective/strong** tacit (community judgment) is un-capturable in principle — but the framework already relocates it to foundational dispositions (D-only) + the program-level emergent property, rather than scoring it per course.

**Four proposed doc-implications (to be tested for justification/overreach):**
1. Make the tacit-fluency reading of D4–D5 explicit in §4/§6.
2. Elevate the §7 text-poor-assessment bias to a named "tacit-knowledge measurement limit" + tie the instructor-confirmation mitigation to it; flag craft courses as priority for the non-text probe.
3. Let *axis* inform the durability model (§6/§15 hyp. B): decay K faster than D.
4. Prioritize "performance under novel/varied conditions" evidence (also the hardest for AI to counterfeit — §16).

**Honesty caveats from pass 1:** SECI is a *management heuristic, not validated cognitive science* (Gourlay critique) — lean on ACT-R/Dreyfus for mechanism, cite SECI only as vocabulary. The operator's claim is one-directional (omits the reflection return-trip §4 requires). The Q5 rebuttal rests on an *inference* from behavior (inherits §7's keyword-inference + text-poor biases) — a good answer to the tension, not a dissolution of it.

**Citations pass 1 flagged (open unless noted):** Polanyi *Tacit Dimension* 1966 [book]; Collins *Tacit & Explicit Knowledge* 2010 [book — the taxonomy that makes Q5 precise]; Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995 [book]; **Anderson 1982** *Psych Review* [OPEN — downloaded]; Dreyfus & Dreyfus 1980/1986; Kolb 1984; Schön 1983; **Gourlay** SECI critique [open]; procedural-memory durability (general consensus); Bahrick permastore + Sinha & Kapur (already in `background.html`).

## 2. Deep-dive verdict (three adversarial Opus agents + acquired papers)

Lit-search + acquisition, adversarial skeptic, and proponent/directional agents. Open PDFs downloaded to gitignored `/research-papers/tacit-knowledge/` (Anderson 1982, Tsoukas 2003, Gottfredson 2003, Ericsson 1993, Gourlay, Christian SJT meta-analysis, procedural-learning ABM 2024). The skeptic and proponent **converge** on the shape; they diverge on exactly one claim, which decides it.

### 2a. Overall verdict — mostly CONFIRMATORY, not directional
KUD+ already operationalizes everything tacit-knowledge theory would *do* (as opposed to *name*): the **Do** axis is already a behavioral competence-under-novelty gradient (so "D4–D5 = tacit fluency" changes no score); **§4** already contains the tacit→explicit reflection return-trip the operator's one-directional "explicit→tacit" framing omits; **§16** already banks the AI-counterfeit-resistance of the deployment anchors; **§7** already names the text-poor bias, its instructor-confirmation mitigation, and the non-text probe (which the proponent confirmed is *built* in `capture-chat-agent.md`). The deep dive's value is **diagnostic reassurance** — the framework held up under a different literature — not a construction project. The operator's "experiential learning moves explicit→tacit" claim is real and mechanistically underpinned (ACT-R proceduralization; Dreyfus), but it is *half a loop* and the framework already closes the other half.

### 2b. Corrections the review forced (things NOT to do)
- **Claim 4 (axis-based durability) is REJECTED — and the initial pass had it backwards.** It claimed "decay K faster than D — validates §6 strongly." But §6's own durability anchor is **Bahrick's permastore, a study of *declarative* (K-type) memory** surviving *decades*. "Decay K faster than D" therefore *contradicts* the citation it claimed to strengthen. Durability tracks **spaced/varied reinforcement past a repetition threshold** (which §6 already models), not the K/U/D axis; the lab procedural-durability asymmetry is confounded with reps (procedural skills are practiced to automaticity). Adding an axis multiplier would **double-count reinforcement** and make the future durability model (§15 hyp. B) measurably wrong. Do not add. (Proponent's "defensible modeling prior" over-weighted the lab asymmetry; skeptic's Bahrick catch is decisive.)
- **SECI stays OUT of `background.html` in any load-bearing role.** Gourlay (empirically anecdotal, inconclusive operationalizations) + **Tsoukas** (the tacit↔explicit "conversion" is a *category mistake* — tacit always underlies explicit; you cannot convert one stock into the other) mean a contested management 2×2 would *lower* the doc's evidence standard at its most careful seam. Lean the mechanism on ACT-R/Dreyfus/Ericsson; if the dissociation cases are ever glossed, frame them as **focal/subsidiary imbalance** (Tsoukas), not "conversions caught mid-cycle."
- **Do not claim to measure "tacit knowledge" as a construct.** The only validated tacit-measurement tradition (Sternberg's tacit-knowledge inventories) is scenario/SJT-based, domain- and expert-standard-bound, and — per Gottfredson and the SJT meta-analyses (Christian; McDaniel) — does *not* isolate a distinct "practical intelligence" factor (it correlates ~.46 with g). The defensible claim is narrower and stronger: **the Do axis measures the behavioral *shadow* of tacit fluency (performance under novelty), which is the only thing anyone validly measures anyway.**

### 2c. What is genuinely earned (both agents converge)
1. **§7 fold-in — APPLIED this pass.** Named the text-poor + keyword-inference biases as jointly **tacitness-correlated** (Collins' *somatic/collective* taxonomy — not SECI), noted the **portfolio-validity literature empirically endorses** the instructor-confirmation + rubric mitigation (craft/portfolio assessment is valid *with* defined rubric + multiple assessors + outcome alignment = the tool's design), and added the **"floor, not ceiling"** reading for low-D craft cells on text-poor evidence.
2. **One candidate PRODUCT change (NOT built — gated):** a persistent **"evidence-limited D" provenance flag** — when a Technical performance-Do competency scores low-D (≤2) on materials-only/absent D-evidence, mark it "floor, not ceiling" downstream, so "genuinely shallow" isn't silently conflated with "assessed through a channel the evidence rule can't quote." This is the *only* candidate that is (a) a real product change, (b) not already built, (c) aimed at the framework's own admitted, tacitness-correlated bias, and (d) landing on GC's crown-jewel skills. It's a read-time provenance derivation consistent with the existing "integer judgment immutable, derived/confidence layer separate" rule — it changes **no** K/U/D score.

### 2d. Lower-value fold-ins — deferred, NOT applied (avoid over-folding, per the skeptic)
- §16: codified-vs-tacit vocabulary + a labor-market corroboration (returns to experience rising in AI-exposed occupations because AI replicates codified but not tacit knowledge — proponent cited a Dallas Fed 2026 piece). **Verify the specific cite before adding** to an evidence-anchored doc; add only as *relative/current* corroboration, never "inherently uncounterfeitable."
- §6: a one-sentence "D4–D5 ≈ proceduralized/adaptive expertise (ACT-R/Dreyfus)" footnote — pure convergent vocabulary, adds no score; the §7 addition already carries the operationally-relevant framing.
- §5: dissociations-as-focal/subsidiary aside — explanatory only, steers nothing.

### 2e. The gate + cheapest first step
Run a **read-only diagnostic** over the captured snapshot corpus: *how many Technical (T1/T2) performance-Do competencies scored low-D (≤2) rest on materials-only or absent D-evidence (no `instructor`/`transcript` citation)?* That quantifies the bias's real footprint from data already stored — mirroring this project's own "gate the build on a cheap diagnostic" discipline (skill-fabric, durability model). **Large footprint → build the "evidence-limited D" flag; small → the §7 caveat is genuinely inert and the honest move is the footnote just applied, nothing more.** The go/no-go is recorded in STATE regardless of outcome.

### 2f. Directional impact — the honest answer
**None to what the tool scores, how, or toward what end** — the framework already encodes the operationally-real content, and the review actively prevented two *wrong* additions (backwards axis-durability; SECI in an evidence-anchored doc). The tacit lens's genuine contribution to *direction* is not a new theory of learning but the sharpened realization that **the framework's one admitted measurement seam (text-poor craft) is exactly where GC's highest-value skills live** — which the §7 fold-in now names, and which the gated "evidence-limited D" flag would surface. Everything else is confirmation.
