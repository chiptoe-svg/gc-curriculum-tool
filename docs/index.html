GC Curriculum & Careers

Working documents for the GC curriculum and careers project. Start with the careers papers (1) on how AI is changing the work GC graduates go into. Then the curriculum section (2): a three-minute brief, the curriculum wiki, and an interactive map that traces careers back to the courses that build them. The background and research section (3) covers the tool’s vision, the framework behind it, and the research it draws on. After that come the faculty guide (4), early interface previews (5) and the design history (6).

1 · AI and GC careers

1a · Careers paperAI and GC CareersWhat the evidence says about AI and early careers, what it means for the markets GC graduates enter, and how the GC curriculum fits now and needs to change. 1b · Careers paperDemand Elasticity and AI JobsWhen AI makes work faster, whether jobs grow or shrink depends on how much more of the output buyers want once it gets cheaper. What is known about that response, where the published numbers come from, and what it suggests for GC's markets. 1c · Proposal · Draft for discussionDesign and Production SystemsThe creative industry is shifting from making finished pieces to building the systems that create them. This proposal suggests how GC could prepare students to build those systems, from strategy and design through to production.

2 · Curriculum

2a · Executive brief · 3-minute readThe GC Curriculum Tool — Executive BriefWhat the tool does and why, the framework it implements, how the faculty workflow runs, where the trial period stands. Written to be shareable with deans, advisory-board members, accreditation reviewers, and peer-program directors. 2b · Curriculum wiki · Read-only copyThe GC Curriculum WikiOne page per captured course, competency and career target, built from the approved course captures. Each claim points back to the capture it came from. 2c · Curriculum map · InteractiveGC Curriculum MapWork back from a career to the competencies it relies on and the courses that build them. Click a course to compare its stated objectives with what its capture found.

3 · Background and research

3a · VisionThe GC Curriculum Tool — VisionThe high-level picture: the two questions the tool answers, what it produces, the 3-Act program structure, the planned end state, and illustrative visualizations. 3b · BackgroundThe KUD+ Framework — Background and JustificationThe academic companion to the Vision: what KUD+ measures, the theoretical traditions it draws on (Backward Design, Constructive Alignment, Bloom's, Competency-Based Education), the depth-scale extension and its evidence rule, foundational competency treatment, and course- and career-path implementation. 3c · ValidationGraduate Outcome ValidationProposal for empirical validation of the framework's output against external occupational data using 1,337 validated Clemson GC graduates, whose 672 first career jobs in 2016–2026 span 103 occupations. 3d · Research deep diveProblem-Solving as a Program-Level Emergent PropertyDeep treatment of the research on how problem-solving competence actually develops in undergraduate education — domain-specificity, productive failure (with Kapur's 2×2 framework and the domain-general-skills caveat), reflection, transfer — and how the KUD+ framework operationalizes that view into per-course audit probes, a program coverage lens, and a scaffolding analysis (Stage 1 live). Companion to Background §8. 3e · Research deep diveThe 3-Act Program StructureHonest accounting of what curriculum-design literature the GC program's 3-Act structure (Foundations & Agency → Integration & Mastery → Specialty & Application) is consistent with, what it isn't grounded in, and how it engages the strongest critiques (Lave & Wenger on situated learning, Schön on artificial boundaries, the prerequisite-transfer problem). Includes corrections from an adversarial review — Flexner history, SDT-vs-self-regulated-learning, brittle-scaffold provenance. Companion to Vision §3-Act. 3f · Research deep diveCurriculum Measurement — Open Hypotheses & Research AgendaThree linked hypotheses about what the KUD+ depth framework measures and how: a durable-depth model, a competency-to-career cross-walk, and a way to handle measurement uncertainty. Forward-looking and not yet validated; most wait on the graduate outcome study. Companion to Background. 3g · Mechanics deep diveHow the Competency Catalog WorksHow the shared competency catalog sits between what courses build and what careers ask: per-competency scoring of course snapshots, how career targets claim competencies, the two merges, alumni occupation mixes, the O*NET 31.0 store, the business-acumen competencies, what is planned next (links, weights, readiness) and the honest limits. 3h · ArchitectureHow the GC Curriculum Tool Works — Architecture OverviewThe system as a system: the KUD+ framework and its evidence rules, the two sides of capture (what courses develop, what careers demand) and the shared competency catalog that joins them, the CourseCapture pipeline, provenance, every infrastructure piece, and a claim-by-claim account of which parts of the framework the code enforces. 3i · Research deep diveCurriculum Maps: Research and the Builder’s DesignWhat the research says about how curriculum maps are built and used, including the difference between what a course states and what students do. Lists which rules in the GC curriculum builder are supported by that research, which are the department’s own choices, and which are assumptions. 3j · Design conceptSkill-String Curriculum MapLinking each course’s required skills to the earlier skills that supply them, with catalog competencies as the evidence and objectives set with Bloom’s taxonomy. Partly built: required-skill statuses and locked strings are live.
