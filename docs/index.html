Research deep dive
Problem-Solving as a Program-Level Emergent Property

Deep treatment of the research on how problem-solving competence actually develops in undergraduate education — domain-specificity, productive failure (with Kapur's 2×2 framework and the domain-general-skills caveat), reflection, transfer — and how the KUD+ framework operationalizes that view into per-course audit probes, a program coverage lens, and a scaffolding analysis (Stage 1 live). Companion to Background §8.
Research deep dive
The 3-Act Program Structure

Honest accounting of what curriculum-design literature the GC program's 3-Act structure (Foundations & Agency → Integration & Mastery → Specialty & Application) is consistent with, what it isn't grounded in, and how it engages the strongest critiques (Lave & Wenger on situated learning, Schön on artificial boundaries, the prerequisite-transfer problem). Includes corrections from an adversarial review — Flexner history, SDT-vs-self-regulated-learning, brittle-scaffold provenance. Companion to Vision §3-Act.
Research deep dive
Curriculum Measurement — Open Hypotheses & Research Agenda

Three linked hypotheses about what the KUD+ depth framework measures and how: a durable-depth model, a competency-to-career cross-walk, and a way to handle measurement uncertainty. Forward-looking and not yet validated; most wait on the graduate outcome study. Companion to Background.
Mechanics deep dive
How the Competency Catalog Works

How the shared competency catalog sits between what courses build and what careers ask: per-competency scoring of course snapshots, how career targets claim competencies, the two merges, alumni occupation mixes, the O*NET 31.0 store, the business-acumen competencies, what is planned next (links, weights, readiness) and the honest limits.
Architecture
How the GC Curriculum Tool Works — Architecture Overview

The system as a system: the KUD+ framework and its evidence rules, the two sides of capture (what courses develop, what careers demand) and the shared competency catalog that joins them, the CourseCapture pipeline, provenance, every infrastructure piece, and a claim-by-claim account of which parts of the framework the code enforces.
