# Soderstrom & Bjork (2015) — Learning Versus Performance: An Integrative Review

**Full citation:** Soderstrom, N. C., & Bjork, R. A. (2015). Learning versus performance: An integrative review. *Perspectives on Psychological Science*, 10(2), 176–199. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000

**Reference ID in background.html:** `ref-soderstrom-bjork-2015`; cited in §15 (research agenda: targeted AI practice for a measured prerequisite gap).

**Where it lives:**

**Accessibility:** Full text held.

**What the background doc claims it says:** Performance during instruction is often an unreliable index of whether long-term learning has taken place (p. 176).

**What it actually says (synthesis):** Reviews motor- and verbal-learning evidence that learning can occur without performance change and that performance gains can fail to produce learning, plus metacognitive evidence that learners misread acquisition performance as learning. Companion to Bjork (1994), already cited on the page.

**Verdict:** Consistent.
