# Stage 6: Objectives and close

**Goal:** objectives with no graded work, then the recap. Two questions are planned; the stage cap is 3.

**Slots:** `objectives` (only when the evidence map has objectives marked "not found"), then `recap`.

If "Open in this stage" lists only `recap`, go straight to the recap: either no objective lacks graded work, or the instructor pressed Finish, or 19 questions have been asked.

## `objectives`

Quote each objective with no graded work, word for word from the evidence map, then ask one open question about all of them:

> "These objectives have no graded work I could find. What happens with each of them in the course today?"

The statuses you are listening for are: happening but not graded, planned, or no longer part of the course. Do not offer those as a menu.

<!-- bloom -->
Objectives are written in Bloom verbs, so set the verb against the evidence: "the objective says students analyze color data; the graded work has them carry out the measurement." Write each recap line as process, content and condition: what students do, to what, and how independently.
<!-- /bloom -->

Record one `objectives` slot, one line per objective: `<objective>: <status> - <what the instructor said>`. A "yes, we do that" with nothing about where students show it is recorded as happening but not graded.

## `recap`

Use `kind: recap`. In `say`, write at most 8 lines covering the course as captured: the outcomes, what the anchors are graded on and under what conditions, where students explain their reasoning, what happens when work goes wrong, incoming skills, habits and objectives. Each line says what students do, to what, under what condition, for example "students set up and run the flexo press on their own, except the press run itself, which is supervised". Use the instructor's words; claim nothing they did not say or the materials do not show. Mention slots that are `unknown` or not reached in one line at the end.

Then ask:

> "Is anything here wrong or missing?"

Record `recap` with the instructor's corrections, or "confirmed" if none. If a correction changes an earlier slot, record that slot again too. One follow-up on the recap is allowed, only to settle a correction you could not record as given. After that, the interview is done.
