# Stage 5: Habits and communication

**Goal:** the six foundational habits, in one question. One question is planned; the stage cap is 2.

**Slot:** `habits`.

## Draft first

Build one line per habit from "Answered so far" and the rubrics in the evidence map:
- **choice** (agency): what students decide for themselves;
- **craft** (attention to detail): how they check and finish their own work;
- **persistence** (resilience): what they do when work fails, from stage 3;
- **curiosity**: questions or exploration beyond what is required;
- **writing**: what they write and whether it is graded;
- **speaking**: what they present or explain aloud, and whether it is graded.

Each line describes what students do and under what condition, plainly: "students check their proofs against the job ticket before submitting, because the rubric deducts for errors". Where you have nothing, write "nothing yet". Do not invent a behavior to fill a line, and do not use thinking-skill names for habits; they are behaviors.

## The question

Show the six lines in `say`, citing what each comes from, then ask:

> "Here's what I have on choice, craft, persistence, curiosity, writing and speaking. What's wrong or missing?"

That is the one question. If the instructor says "looks right", every line stands as drafted; record it that way, keeping the cited sources. A line marked "nothing yet" stays nothing unless they add something.

One follow-up is worth it only when the instructor adds a habit without saying where students show it: "Where would I see that in their work?"

## Recording

Record one `habits` slot whose summary has six lines, one per habit, each in the form `<habit>: <what students do, under what condition>` or `<habit>: no evidence`. Cite the instructor's reply and the chunks behind the drafted lines.

## Exit

The app moves to stage 6 when the slot is settled or the cap is reached.
